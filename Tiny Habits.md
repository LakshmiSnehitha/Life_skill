## Question 2
### How can you use B = MAP to make making new habits easier? What are M, A and P?

B = MAP means Behavior happens when Motivation, Ability, and Prompt come together.

- **M = Motivation** → wanting to do something  
- **A = Ability** → being able to do it easily  
- **P = Prompt** → a reminder or trigger to do it  

We can use B = MAP by making habits small and easy. For example, if I want to do coding daily, I can code for just 10 minutes, stay motivated by thinking about improving my skills, and set a daily reminder to practice coding.

---

## Question 3
### Why is it important to "Shine" or Celebrate after each successful completion of habit?

Celebrating after completing a habit is important because it makes our brain feel happy and successful. This positive feeling helps the brain remember the habit and makes us want to repeat it again. Small celebrations like saying “Good job!” or smiling can help build habits faster.

# Atomic Habits

## Question 5
### What is the book's perspective about Identity?

The book says that real change starts with identity. Instead of saying “I want to run,” we should say “I am a runner.” Habits become stronger when we believe they are part of who we are.

---

## Question 6
### Write about the book's perspective on how to make a habit easier to do?

The book says habits become easier when we make them small, simple, and attractive. We should reduce effort, prepare the environment, and start with tiny actions like reading one page or coding for 10 minutes daily.

---

## Question 7
### Write about the book's perspective on how to make a habit harder to do?

The book says bad habits become harder when we increase difficulty and remove triggers. For example, keeping the phone away while studying or deleting distracting apps can help stop bad habits.

## Question 8
### Pick one habit that you would like to do more of? What are the steps that you can take to make the cue obvious or the habit more attractive or easy and/or the response satisfying?

One habit I would like to do more of is daily coding practice.

- **Make the cue obvious:** Keep my laptop ready and set a fixed coding time every day.
- **Make it attractive:** Practice interesting coding problems and track my progress.
- **Make it easy:** Start with just 10 minutes or one easy problem daily.
- **Make it satisfying:** Celebrate small wins and feel proud after completing coding practice.

---

## Question 9
### Pick one habit that you would like to eliminate or do less of? What are the steps that you can take to make the cue invisible or the process unattractive or hard or the response unsatisfying?

One habit I would like to reduce is using my phone too much.

- **Make the cue invisible:** Keep my phone away while studying or coding.
- **Make it unattractive:** Think about how it wastes time and reduces focus.
- **Make it hard:** Turn off notifications or uninstall distracting apps.
- **Make it unsatisfying:** Track wasted screen time and remind myself of missed goals.
