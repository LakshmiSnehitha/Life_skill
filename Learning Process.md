The Feynman Technique is a learning method where you understand something deeply by explaining it in very simple words, like teaching a child.

## 3. What are active and diffused modes of thinking?

### Focused Mode Thinking
Focused mode thinking means concentrating deeply on one problem or topic, like solving a coding problem or studying Java.

### Diffused Mode Thinking
Diffused mode thinking means relaxing the brain and letting ideas connect freely, like when walking, showering, or resting, and suddenly getting a solution.
