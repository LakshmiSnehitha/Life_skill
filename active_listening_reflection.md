# Listening and Active Communication

## Active Listening
### Strategies
- Clear my mind and listen carefully to the speaker.
- Avoid interrupting while the other person is talking.
- Ask relevant questions to better understand the conversation.
- Show interest through positive body language and eye contact.
- Take notes during important conversations when necessary.

## Reflective Listening
### Key Points
- Have the right mindset by avoiding judgment and trying to understand others’ perspectives.
- Speak less and listen more.
- Focus more on the speaker’s feelings rather than only the facts.
- Match the tone and vibe of the conversation naturally.

## Reflection
- Sometimes, when people speak about topics that do not interest me, my attention drifts away, and I miss important information from the conversation.
- To improve my listening skills, I am planning to listen to podcasts regularly for 10–15 minutes, even on topics that may not initially interest me.

## Types of Communication
- I sometimes avoid expressing my opinions because I feel speaking up may create unnecessary problems. For example, when I go out with friends and they order something I do not like, I usually stay silent even if it bothers me.
- When I feel stressed, I tend to become more rude or impatient in conversations.
- I usually react this way when I feel angry, frustrated, or dissatisfied with someone or something.

## Steps for Improvement
- Avoid judging people based only on their opinions.
- Maintain better eye contact during conversations.
- Pay attention to my body language and facial expressions.
- Practice expressing my thoughts politely and confidently.
- Stay calm and think before responding, especially during stressful situations.
