# Growth Mindset Notes

## 1. Paraphrase (summarize) the video in a few (1 or 2) lines. Use your own words.

The video explains that success is not only about talent or intelligence, but mostly about grit, which means having passion and perseverance to continue working toward long-term goals even after failures and challenges.

---

## 2. Paraphrase (summarize) the video in a few (1 or 2) lines in your own words.

The video explains that a growth mindset means believing that skills and intelligence can improve through learning, practice, and effort, while a fixed mindset believes abilities cannot change. It also shows that people with a growth mindset learn from mistakes, accept challenges, and keep improving instead of giving up.

---

## 3. What is the Internal Locus of Control? What is the key point in the video?

Internal Locus of Control means believing that your actions and efforts can change your results.

The main point of the video is that we stay motivated when we focus on things we can control, like our work, attitude, and habits.

---

## 4. What are the key points mentioned by the speaker to build a growth mindset? (Explanation not needed)

- Believe that skills can improve with practice  
- Accept challenges  
- Learn from mistakes and failures  
- Replace negative thoughts with positive thinking  
- Keep learning new things  
- Take feedback positively  

---

## 5. What are your ideas to take action and build a Growth Mindset?

- Practice coding daily without giving up  
- Learn from mistakes and errors  
- Stay calm and focused while solving problems  
- Use Google, documentation, and Stack Overflow to learn independently  
- Understand concepts clearly before writing code  
- Take every challenge as a learning opportunity  
- Improve code by making it readable, modular, and efficient  
- Stay confident and positive in difficult situations  
- Keep repeating practice to achieve mastery  
- Take responsibility for projects and complete them properly
